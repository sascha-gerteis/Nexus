Core message: **AI is not the product. The output is.**

Audience: agency owners, small-business operators, marketing managers and teams that want usable results without learning automation tools.

Primary campaign offer: a free Social Media Report for selected founding customers, followed by the Online Visibility Reporting Bundle when the business wants recurring, broader intelligence.
